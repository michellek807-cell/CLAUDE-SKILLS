## STEP 3: Six clips in Seedance 2.5 (Reference mode, 10 seconds each, 16:9)

**Settings for every clip:** Seedance 2.5 · Reference mode (not first-frame) · 16:9 · 10 seconds.
**Upload order matters.** Image 1 is always the **STYLE KEY**. For clips 1, 4 and 5, upload `refs/REF_C_left_shoe_AFTER_lift.jpg` second, so it becomes Image 2.

Why these prompts look different from the 2.0 version:
- Seedance 2.5 reads **second-by-second timestamps**, so each clip is a timed beat sheet.
- It only obeys negatives for **subtitles and audio**. Everything else (no logos, no watch, lips closed) is now written as a positive instruction in the consistency notes.
- Clips are set to **no background music**. You add one continuous music bed in CapCut, so the music doesn't change key at every cut.
- Every clip repeats the shoe and character details in its last beat, because 2.5 tends to drift near the end of a clip.

Why 6 × 10s and not 2 × 30s: each clip matches one voiceover line, and if one clip fails you only regenerate 10 seconds instead of 30.
