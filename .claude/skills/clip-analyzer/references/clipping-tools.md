# Clipping tool fingerprints

Use this to identify a tool from a screen recording or from the look of a finished clip. Feature lists describe what each tool is known for. Plans and limits change often, so verify pricing on the official site before quoting it.

## How to spot the tool

| Clue in the clip | Likely tool |
|---|---|
| Dashboard with a "virality score" badge on each clip, auto-hook text at top of clip | Opus Clip |
| Purple-leaning UI, "Clips" tab next to "Subtitles" and "Social", B-roll suggestions | Vizard |
| Clips grouped by topic with a "Topics" list, yellow-green highlight captions | Klap |
| Caption styles named after creators (Hormozi-style boxes, bold word pops), emoji auto-insert | Submagic or CapCut auto-captions |
| Timeline editor with text-based editing where deleting a word deletes the audio | Descript |
| "Magic Clips" button inside a podcast recording studio | Riverside |
| Clips appear inside a scheduling calendar with a "Publish" button | Munch, Vizard, or Opus Clip (all have schedulers) |
| TikTok-native editing, templates, trending sounds, free | CapCut |
| A payout dashboard, campaign cards, "earn per 1k views" | A clipping marketplace (Whop clipping campaigns and similar), not an editing tool |

## Feature map by category

### Auto-clippers (long video in, many shorts out)

Core behavior: ingest a YouTube link or upload, transcribe, score segments, cut 15 to 90 s clips, auto-reframe to 9:16, burn captions, add an AI hook title.

| Tool | Strengths | Weaknesses to watch |
|---|---|---|
| Opus Clip | Fast, strong hook titles, team workspace, brand templates | Hooks can be clickbaity, reframe loses the second speaker |
| Vizard | Good speaker tracking, built-in B-roll, scheduler | Caption styling less flexible than CapCut |
| Klap | Topic grouping makes curation faster | Fewer caption styles |
| Munch | Keyword and trend scoring, repurposes to text posts too | Dated UI, slower processing |
| 2short.ai | Simple, cheap entry point | Fewer templates, quality varies |

### Caption and polish tools (clip in, finished clip out)

| Tool | What it adds |
|---|---|
| CapCut (desktop and mobile) | Free auto-captions, templates, keyframes, trending sounds, direct TikTok post. Best place to apply the MamaCarrie caption style by hand. |
| Submagic | Creator-style captions, auto-zoom, auto B-roll, SFX on cuts |
| Descript | Text-based editing, filler-word removal, studio sound, eye contact fix |
| Captions app | Mobile-first, AI eye contact, dubbing |

### Where clipping work comes from

| Source | How it pays | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Creator or podcaster hires you directly | Per clip or monthly retainer | Best margin, requires outreach |
| Clipping campaigns on marketplaces | Per 1,000 views on your reposts, with a cap | Read the brand rules closely, payouts are view-verified and can be rejected |
| Your own channels (MamaCarrie, How Medications Are Made) | Platform monetization and funnel to products | Slow start, full ownership |

## Caption style presets to recognize

- **Word pop**: one to three words at a time, bold sans, white with a colored active word. High retention, fast feel.
- **Karaoke**: full phrase shown, active word highlighted. Easier to read, calmer feel. Good fit for MamaCarrie.
- **Boxed**: words sit on colored boxes. Loud, business-content feel.
- **Lower-third sentence**: full sentence at the bottom. Documentary feel. Good fit for How Medications Are Made.

## MamaCarrie caption spec (apply in CapCut or any tool with custom styles)

- Font: a clean serif or elegant sans, never a comic or bubble font.
- Base text: cream #F3E9D8. Active word: gold #C9A24A. Background block if used: burgundy #6B1F2A at 85 percent opacity.
- Position: lower third, above the platform UI safe zone (keep the bottom 20 percent clear).
- Pace: phrase captions, 3 to 5 words per card.

## How Medications Are Made caption spec

- Font: neutral sans (Inter, Helvetica, or the platform default).
- White text, thin dark outline or soft shadow, no color highlights.
- Lower-third sentences, source name on screen when a fact is cited.
